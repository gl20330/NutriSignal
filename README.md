## Overview
NutriSignal is a mobile application designed to help users make more informed and confident food choices while grocery shopping. The app simplifies nutrition information by providing clear health ratings, ingredient insights, and product comparisons.

This prototype demonstrates three core user tasks:
- Scanning and understanding ingredient lists  
- Comparing two products side-by-side  
- Building a shopping list and receiving an overall cart health score

## How to Use the Prototype

### Starting Point
- Open the prototype link  
- Begin on the home screen  

### Task 1: Ingredient List Scan
- Click **“Scan Ingredient List”**  
- Simulate scanning a product’s ingredient label  
- View flagged ingredients (e.g., processed ingredients, allergens)  
- Click **“Learn More”** to see detailed explanations  

### Task 2: Smart Compare
- Click **“Smart Compare”**  
- Select a goal (e.g., lower sugar, higher protein), or "Skip, just compare"
- Scan Product A and Product B  
- View a side-by-side comparison of nutritional values  
- Review the recommended product and reasoning  

### Task 3: Shopping List & Cart Analysis
- Click **“Smart Cart”**  
- Use the search page to add items to the list (preloaded items)  
- View your shopping list and adjust quantities  
- Click **“Analyze Cart”**  
- View overall cart health score and recommendations
